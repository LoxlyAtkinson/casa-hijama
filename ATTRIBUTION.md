# Human body mesh attribution

The interactive body guide uses `public/assets/body-hubmap-skin.glb` and `public/assets/body-hubmap-pelvis.glb`, the **VH_M_Skin.glb** and **VH_M_Pelvis.glb** reference objects from the HuBMAP CCF 3D Reference Object Library, version 1.2. The pelvis file supplies only a subdued sacrum detail in the lower-back view. Fine scan lines and the cyan glow are procedural surface shading on the rotating skin mesh.

All local GLBs are unmodified copies of the source meshes. In the browser they are scaled, translated, rotated, rendered with a translucent cyan clinical shader and a soft pink region focus, and combined with Casa Hijama's abstract internal geometry. These display adaptations do not imply anatomical precision or medical diagnosis. A source credit and license link also appear in the body guide.
