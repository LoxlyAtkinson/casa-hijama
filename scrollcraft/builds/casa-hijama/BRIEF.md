# Casa Hijama Scroll Craft brief

## Source and authority

User supplied the Casa Hijama website, Apple's Watch Series 12 page as a concrete motion reference, a product showcase image, and a clinical body-dashboard reference. User requested the existing brand colors, a dark mode, a real 3D body, scroll-led exploration, and a live public preview. The section sequence below is authored from the current site's required content and those requests; it is not a user quotation.

## Eight topics

1. Vibe: calm, premium, clinical, welcoming (inferred from references and Casa identity). Reference: Apple Watch Series 12; supplied VITA body visual; supplied three-state shop image.
2. Journey: atmospheric Cape Town introduction → studio/tradition → treatment choices → interactive body exploration → private-room experience → social proof and prices → product support → booking and location.
3. Energy: inviting open, informative approach, an intentionally quiet breath before the body section, high-engagement body peak, calm studio release, practical finish.
4. Feeling curve: welcome from hero scene; trust from real care/studio; clarity from distinct treatment imagery; agency from the 3D body and questions; reassurance from private room; confidence from reviews/prices; readiness from booking.
5. Signature move: click a pain area on a scroll-rotating clinical body, then clarify context in a short optional questionnaire without diagnostic output.
6. Range: premium minimal with visible motion and cinematic transitions, rather than a static brochure (inferred from repeated user feedback).
7. Structure: distinct chapters/scenes, not one continuous flight (authored decision based on four existing routes and multiple content types).
8. Assets: current Casa brand palette and product photos, generated editorial treatment/room imagery, a Higgsfield room film, generated clinical fallback imagery, and a procedural Three.js body.

## Visitor and outcome

For a Cape Town visitor considering Hijama, dry cupping, massage, or products. By the end, they should understand the options and feel able to ask Casa Hijama about suitability. Primary action: **Book a session**. Product enquiries remain separate.

## Peak and tell-someone sentence

Peak: “I scrolled into a clinical body, turned it to the area that bothers me, and left with better questions for my therapist.” It lives in the body guide and receives the longest deliberate scroll span.

It's the site where a body model turns with the story and helps you prepare a thoughtful conversation before booking.

## Authored silence

The quote after the room film is intentionally calm and static so the visitor can read it. The price and booking sections prioritize accuracy and form usability over decorative motion.
