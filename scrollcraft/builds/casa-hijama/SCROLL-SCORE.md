# Page grammar and scroll score

Grammar: distinct editorial chapters. A single filmic one-shot would obscure practical treatment, pricing, shop and booking content. The signature move is the 3D body consultation guide. The fingerprint registry was empty on this project's first Scroll Craft invocation.

| Beat | Feeling | Device | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening hero | Welcome | Layered photographic parallax and headline settle | Establish Cape Town studio depth while preserving immediate CTA |
| Studio and tradition | Trust | Image reveal and restrained scroll parallax | Let real setting replace hero atmosphere |
| Treatment choices | Clarity | Lateral or staggered image-led choice reveal | Distinguish wet, dry and massage at a glance |
| Interactive body | Agency, peak | Long pinned 3D camera orbit and focus | Scroll and clicks advance understanding; questionnaire grounds the interaction |
| Private room | Reassurance | Scroll-scrubbed short Higgsfield film | Move from clinical exploration into the visit atmosphere |
| Quote and reviews | Calm proof | Static quote followed by soft reveal | Give reading and testimony room |
| Prices, products, booking | Confidence and commitment | Staged cards, then static form | Keep price and form details stable and usable |

Opening: full headline and CTA over a photographic room/Cape Town layer. Midpoint: body turns to show the relevant back area and focuses the active marker. Exit: the model resolves, private room film takes over, then practical content leads to booking.

Mobile is separately composed: shorter visual travel, readable body copy, visible area controls, no sideways overflow. Reduced motion shows static assets and all information in ordinary flow.
