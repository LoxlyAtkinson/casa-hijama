Scope: `design-system.md` against the live local home page at `http://127.0.0.1:4173/` and the supplied v6 screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844, including `v6-state-continuity/mobile-dark-insets.png`. ## Binary verdict

- **Home: PASS.** At 1440px and 390px, the sticky branded header, calm turquoise/ivory identity, Manrope-led hierarchy, contrasting cup and room scenes, and visible booking/exploration actions match the system. The mobile hero and room beat retain readable copy and action bounds in both supplied states.
- **Body: FAIL.** The 100% cyan figure is complete and recognizable on desktop and mobile; coral focus, back rotation, light/dark contrast, and the 200% neck/lower-back labels read clearly without crossing the zoom rail. The mobile composition still pushes the primary region action and treatment link below the 390×844 captured frame. In `mobile-body-front.png`, `mobile-body-upper.png`, `mobile-body-lower.png`, both mobile 200% captures, and the dark inset, the section reaches the viewport bottom while body copy continues and neither action appears. The desktop equivalents place both actions beside the current guidance. The mobile intake capture likewise cuts the review action at the bottom edge, so its initial action bound is not fully visible. These captures do not establish that those actions are impossible to reach by further scrolling; they establish that the primary next action is missing from the visible mobile chapter composition. That fails the system's clear next-action and responsive section requirements.

**Biggest objective gap:** At 390×844, fit the body guidance and its primary action into a complete, readable chapter view, or make the action's reachable continuation visually explicit. Recapture normal, dark, 200% zoom, and open intake states showing the full action bounds.
