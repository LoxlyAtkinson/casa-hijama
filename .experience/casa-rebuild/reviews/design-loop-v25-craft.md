Scope: fresh visual craft judgment of the home opening and live 3D body scene. I used only `bar-v3.md`, the specified v6 captures, `v6-state-continuity/mobile-dark-insets.png`, the user-supplied body image, the live Apple Watch Series 12 page, and the local rendered page at `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`. This is visual craft judgment, not a claim that interaction or accessibility gates pass.

## Home opening — PASS

The first frame gives the glass cup clear image priority, with controlled cyan/amber lighting and legible booking action. Desktop uses a large subject beside a short headline; mobile preserves the cup as the dominant subject before the type. The room scroll state changes subject and mood, rather than merely fading text. The quiet room photograph forms an intelligible second beat. Against the live Apple page's near-black macro opening and scale change, this is a credible Casa-specific translation, with enough restraint and finish to pass this scoped craft bar.

## Live 3D body — FAIL

The figure is continuous, cyan, rotatable in the saved views, and uses one active pink focus. Its full-body front and back compositions are readable. **Single biggest fixable gap:** the 200% region views do not deliver convincing anatomical detail. The shoulder view enlarges simple rib and clavicle strokes; the lower-back view enlarges ring-like marks and a broad glow. The soft fade then removes surrounding anatomy, so zoom feels like enlarging a translucent mannequin rather than entering a clinical scan. The reference earns its spatial detail with clearly resolved bones/joints in the regional views. Build one selected-region detail treatment tied to the actual figure, with recognizable anatomical structure and a crisp focal hierarchy at 200%, while retaining the explanatory HTML. The current pixels do not meet that part of the bar.

The `mobile-dark-insets.png` state keeps the back figure and text readable in dark mode, but the figure still lacks a resolved regional detail view. Dark mode does not change the body verdict.
