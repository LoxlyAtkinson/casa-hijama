# Design Loop v15 — independent craft critic

**Verdict: FAIL**

Reviewed 2026-10-17 against `bar-v3.md` and the rendered `evidence/v6` PNGs at their original resolution. This is a visual craft judgment of the supplied captures, not a source or live interaction audit. The strongest Apple-aligned work is the two-scene cup → private-room hero: each scene has one dominant subject, concise large type, a clear action, and an effective dark-to-warm change. The body scene has a readable three-chapter structure and its front/back/full-turn screenshots demonstrate distinct views. The remaining gaps below are visible and achievable.

## Biggest gap — live figure falls below its own still

The live figure in `desktop-body-front.png`, `desktop-body-upper.png`, `mobile-body-front.png`, and `mobile-body-upper.png` reads as a glowing solid mannequin. The smooth featureless face, blunt shoulder/hip volumes, and prominent groin shape make it more human-dummy-like than the finer, translucent clinical scan shown in `mobile-body-fallback.png` and `mobile-body-reduced.png`. The still has a slender silhouette, visible internal structure and layered illumination; the live body has only faint surface lines. The disparity is immediately apparent in the same 390px body stage. This misses the luminous clinical quality and comfortable synthetic anatomy in bar mechanisms 5–6 and contract C23/C29. Refine the live material and silhouette so its inner lines, cyan translucency and edge light approach the still; soften the face and pelvic modeling. Retain a single restrained coral focus.

## Other visible defects

1. **Zoom framing cuts the figure abruptly.** In `desktop-body-neck-zoom.png` the arms terminate at straight stage edges and the torso dissolves into a dark band; `desktop-body-lower-zoom.png` cuts the head across the shoulder line and fades the legs before the stage ends. The same top/bottom erasure is more prominent in `mobile-body-neck-zoom.png` and `mobile-body-lower-zoom.png`. The focus grows, but the fade resembles missing geometry instead of an intentional scan aperture. Use a shaped vignette or tighter purposeful crop around the selected region, with the figure's continuation still legible. This is the largest gap in the selected-area detail mechanism 7.
2. **Mobile stage controls compete with the subject.** At 390px, the tall zoom rail occupies the left side of the figure and the `Turn figure` button lies over the lower body/feet in `mobile-body-front.png`; at 200% the rail abuts the large torso in `mobile-body-neck-zoom.png` and `mobile-body-lower-zoom.png`. The `FRONT VIEW`/`BACK VIEW` caption is squeezed between the rail, model and chapter counter. Move these controls into a coherent strip outside the figure or give the model a reserved safe area. Keep direct area selection available.
3. **Inactive body labels retain too much presence on mobile.** In `mobile-body-upper.png` and `mobile-body-lower.png`, the inactive label sits over the illuminated torso near the active coral label. The model itself is small enough that the two text labels form a competing overlay, weakening the single warm focal point required by bar mechanism 6. Reduce inactive labels to subdued dots, move them outside the body, or reveal their names on focus/selection while the named chapter controls remain below.
4. **The body chapter lacks the hero's scale of visual change.** The hero progresses from macro cup to full room (`desktop-hero-start.png`, `desktop-hero-room.png`). The three body states retain exactly the same full-body framing and three-column composition (`desktop-body-front.png`, `desktop-body-upper.png`, `desktop-body-lower.png`), changing orientation and a small marker. The 200% view is manually available, but no supplied scroll-state frame shows a closer spatial detail as the narrative progresses. A restrained camera push or region-linked detail panel during the upper/lower stops would better meet bar mechanism 2 and C22 without sacrificing the readable controls.

## Evidence limits

The v6 captures and browser log show 1440×900 and 390px states, front/back, drag/full-turn, reverse scroll, zoom, dark, reduced-motion, fallback and intake. Still PNGs cannot establish animation smoothness, actual drag feel, whether a fade is transient, or whether the modal's partially visible bottom action in `mobile-body-keyboard-intake.png` becomes reachable by scrolling. I did not inspect source, prior reviews or builder notes and did not run the page. The verdict is limited to visual craft in these supplied frames; it does not override separate interaction or acceptance gates.
