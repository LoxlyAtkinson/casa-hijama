# Design loop v5, round 3 — craft critic

Scope: `bar-v3.md` and rendered reference/v5 frames only. Static frames establish visible treatment, not live motion or interaction.

## Home — PASS

**Winning treatment:** The cupping glass owns the opening frame. Its near-black field, sharp cyan and amber edge light, restrained headline, and visible booking action translate the Apple reference's dominant macro subject into Casa's own service identity. The room frame is a genuine new scene with a warmer palette and specific privacy message; mobile preserves the visual-first sequence and gives the image room before the copy. This is an editorial visual change, not a text fade.

The desktop headline sits beside the subject rather than in Apple's separate low zone, but the composition works for Casa: it keeps the glass unoccluded and the booking action immediately legible. The pale navigation bar is a more abrupt break from the immersion than Apple's dark chrome, yet it remains sparse and coherent. The supplied frames do not show later section cadence, motion pause, or reduced-motion behavior, so those mechanisms remain unverified by this visual review.

## Body — FAIL

**Winning treatment:** The translucent cyan figure, bright rim, visible spine/rib suggestions, and single pink focus are far closer to the clinical scan reference than a solid mannequin. Front and back selections make the active region unambiguous. The 200% lower-back frame adds real spatial emphasis, and the surrounding explanation correctly frames the region as a discussion area rather than a diagnosis. Mobile keeps direct region choices and readable caution copy. The mobile fallback actually shows the strongest anatomy-like detail of the Casa variants.

**Biggest actionable gap:** The primary figure still reads as a simplified, nearly featureless digital mannequin at normal scale, especially on mobile. Its smooth mask-like head, cylindrical limbs, and schematic internal lines lack the layered, fine anatomy and luminous depth that make the reference read as a scan. Bring the richer anatomical texture and translucency visible in the fallback into the primary rotating figure, then light it so those forms remain legible at the default mobile size. Keep the one active warm focus and the current clinical caution copy. The supplied stills cannot prove whether rotation is real WebGL or whether the fallback and reduced-motion controls behave correctly.
