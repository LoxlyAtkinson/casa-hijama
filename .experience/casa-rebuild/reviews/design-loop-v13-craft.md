# Design Loop v13 — independent craft verdict

**Verdict: FAIL.** The opening cup-to-room sequence is strong: each desktop panel has one clear subject, a large editorial statement, and a materially different scene; the mobile crops keep the subject and copy legible. The body section does not yet reach the supplied cyan clinical reference or the quality of its own reduced-motion/failure still.

**Single biggest gap:** The live figure reads as an opaque cyan mannequin with thin anatomical lines drawn over it. In the supplied reference and in `mobile-body-reduced.png`/`mobile-body-fallback.png`, bright edges, visible internal form and light passing through the silhouette create the clinical-scan impression. The live front/back captures have a broad, nearly uniform torso, faint interior detail, and a weak rim. The luminous pink region is present but does not compensate for the flat body material. This gap is evident at both 1440px and 390px (`desktop-body-front.png`, `desktop-body-upper.png`, `mobile-body-front.png`, `mobile-body-upper.png`). Improve the live material/lighting and internal depth so the real rotatable model itself carries that scan quality; preserve its clearly synthetic appearance.

**Other achievable defects:**

1. The 200% body focus loses a deliberate crop. In `desktop-body-neck-zoom.png` the torso ends in a hard horizontal edge; in `desktop-body-lower-zoom.png` and `mobile-body-lower-zoom.png` the shoulders/head and thighs are abruptly cut. Frame the focused anatomy inside a consistent soft viewport or fade the mesh at its crop boundary, keeping the active point centered and visibly connected to the whole-body state.
2. At 390px the live whole figure is much smaller and dimmer than the still, leaving the body less compelling than its fallback (`mobile-body-front.png` versus `mobile-body-fallback.png`). Increase effective body scale/contrast within the available stage without colliding with the zoom rail or chapter controls.
3. The side-on 200% state (`desktop-body-dark.png`, `mobile-body-dark.png`, `mobile-body-mouse-drag.png`) presents a cut-off head and lower body while the lower-back point sits close to the silhouette edge. The selected anatomy should remain the obvious visual center during a drag or zoom, with enough surrounding form to orient the visitor.

The three live chapter captures do show one continuous recognizably human silhouette, a front/back turn, and distinct coral active points. Inactive points recede. The hero transition changes subject rather than merely fading copy. Those gains are visible, but the live body's material and focused framing remain meaningful craft defects under bar-v3 mechanisms 5–7.
