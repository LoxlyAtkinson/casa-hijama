The active chapter, visible label, focal light and guidance change together. After a back-view release, the next forward-scroll captures show Lower Back, and reverse-scroll captures return to the front Neck chapter. The wiring reanchors the released drag at chapter center, using `(target + .5) / 3` and `jumpTo(index)`; the Upper Back target is therefore **0.50** of the sticky scroll travel. The log calls it “Aligned scroll chapter 0.50.” This supports the outcome, though the log does not contain a measured `scrollY`, progress value or wheel delta. See `desktop-body-{front,turn-to-back,full-turn,drag-then-scroll-forward,drag-then-scroll-reverse}.png` and their mobile equivalents.
2. **Direct and accessible area control pass.** Named region buttons, visible focus points and keyboard Enter are wired to the four-question local intake. The desktop/mobile keyboard-intake captures show the dialog. Zoom captures show 200% front and lower-back focus with legible labels and controls. Pointer movement beyond five pixels suppresses accidental marker selection; the drag capture and log show rotation without a dialog.
3. **Still, reduced-motion and failure paths pass for the body.** The reduced-motion capture shows a static front figure with the guidance in reading order. WebGL-blocked front and back captures retain named regions and a matching clinical still. The live model is a continuous, luminous cyan surface with localized coral focus in the inspected screenshots; this addresses the brief’s functional visual description without making a comparative craft judgment.
4. **Other journeys pass in the older site run.** The 45-observation log covers all four direct routes in 1440px and 390px light/dark states, with reported width parity; 30 products, filtering, sorting, dialog keyboard return, empty/populated bag and accurate quantity/subtotal WhatsApp text; booking validation, pending-confirmation handoff and visible fallback; FAQ keyboard operation, map fallback, film scrub/pause/still, theme persistence and a no-JavaScript catalog.
