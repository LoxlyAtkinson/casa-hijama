# Independent Design Loop craft review — v32

**Home: PASS. Body: FAIL.** Reviewed the user-supplied `3d human body.jpg`, `bar-v3.md`, [Apple Watch Series 12](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch-series-12/), current 06:25–06:28 desktop/mobile captures in `evidence/v6/`, and `evidence/v6-state-continuity/*handoff-mid-v30.png`. I did not read builder rationale, source, or earlier reviews.

## What reads well

- Home opens with one sharply lit cupping subject, a short oversized statement and sparse actions. The first viewport has a clear Apple-like image hierarchy at desktop and mobile sizes; the booking action remains legible.
- The body is now a continuous translucent cyan human figure with one localized warm selected point. Its 100% view reads as a clinical scan rather than a solid mannequin. The 200% neck and lumbar frames materially change the visible anatomy and the circular inset is visibly connected to the selected point by a line. The new round lens avoids the unrelated rectangular tile impression.
- The sampled v30 handoff middle frame is occupied by the same figure on both sides of the reveal boundary; it does not show a blank flash. This is a still-frame visual judgment only.

## Blocking pixel gap

The primary teal **Explore this area** action is visually cropped to roughly **“Expla” plus the arrow** in the latest `mobile-body-lower-zoom.png`, `mobile-body-dark.png`, and `desktop-body-dark.png`. The same button shows its complete label in `mobile-body-front.png` and `mobile-body-neck-zoom.png`. This intermittent missing label makes the selected lower-back state look broken, and it affects the exact action that turns exploration into the intake journey. Keep the full text painted in every selected angle, zoom, theme, and scroll state; recapture those three states to prove it. The apparent cause is not established by these screenshots.

## Secondary craft gap

At 100% the inset is small compared with the body, and its blue/magenta anatomy detail can be hard to interpret without first using 200% zoom. Once the CTA is fixed, a little more emphasis on the selected region and its linked lens at 100% would move it closer to the user’s clinical inset reference. The 200% view already demonstrates the desired spatial detail, so this is a polish issue, not a request for another graphic layer.

## Limits

This is an independent visual review of saved frames, not a motion-performance or control-function test. The v30 handoff midpoint is newer than the old final-live screenshot but may predate the latest 06:28 body captures; continuous playback needs separate direct verification. I inspected Apple’s page content and the documented visual mechanisms in `bar-v3.md`, not a synchronized side-by-side motion recording of the current Apple site.
