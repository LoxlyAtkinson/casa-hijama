## Live body: FAIL

The translucent cyan silhouette, internal ribs and spine, one pink active focus, back turn, and 200% close views are all present. The model remains visually subordinate to its surrounding interface. In the normal mobile front and back frames it is only about 300 px tall in an 844 px viewport, with large dark margins; the supplied clinical image makes the human figure the dominant full-height object. Desktop similarly gives the figure a narrow middle column while the text columns command the screen. The scan reads as a small interactive diagram before it reads as an immersive human body. The dark mobile inset shows the same scale issue at a larger capture size.

**Single biggest fixable pixel gap:** Increase the normal-view figure's apparent scale and luminous anatomical presence, especially on mobile, so it owns the available dark stage while retaining room for the region labels and controls. Aim for a roughly 420–480 px tall mobile figure in the comparable viewport; preserve the existing clear single focus and close framing at 200%.
