# Design loop v5 — independent craft review

Scope: visual judgment from `bar-v3.md`, the supplied Apple and body references, and the twelve named final Casa screenshots only. I did not inspect implementation, interactions, motion playback, or builder rationale. The shared filesystem and supplied static captures limit blinding and behaviour verification.

## Home — FAIL

**Composition winner for the bar's cinematic opening: Apple.** Its single watch is isolated at macro scale against near-black, with the short statement in a separate low zone. Casa's desktop opening has an attractive Cape Town setting and visible glass cups, but the wide scenic backdrop, four similar cups, and very large overlaid heading divide attention. On mobile, the skyline takes much of the upper frame while the cups sit behind the copy. The image never gains the singular, unmistakable subject priority required by item 1.

Casa does demonstrate a meaningful change from cups and panorama to treatment room in both viewport sequences; it is more than a text fade. The room's message and treatment link are readable. The exit frames show an intentional change to a light editorial section, and booking remains visible on desktop and in the mobile start frame. No film pause control is visible in the supplied hero frames; these stills also cannot verify its behaviour or reduced-motion reading.

**Biggest actionable gap:** compose the opening around one dominant, close, unmistakable Casa subject, with the statement in its own quiet zone. Preserve the legible booking path and the room scene change. Recapture desktop and mobile frames that make that hierarchy observable.

## Body — FAIL

**Composition winner for the clinical scan: the supplied body reference.** Casa has a continuous cyan figure on dark blue, a brighter rim, faint rib-like lines, one active warm point, readable HTML explanations, and visible region, zoom, and turn controls. These are useful parts of the bar. The figure is still a small, mostly uniform silhouette in the desktop layout and even smaller on mobile. Its anatomy lacks the layered, luminous spatial depth of the reference. The selected warm focus reads as a tiny labeled dot rather than a localized light within an anatomical area.

The upper and lower selections visibly move the active focus and change the explanatory panel. The `115%` lower-back desktop frame enlarges the whole figure until head and feet are clipped while adding little discernible lower-back detail; the mobile lower-back frame is still a whole-body view. Thus the selected-region view does not deliver the reference's additional spatial detail. Rotation and selection behaviour cannot be established from stills alone.

**Biggest actionable gap:** make selection reveal a genuine, model-connected anatomical close view of the chosen region. Increase the figure's dimensional translucency and internal detail so the highlighted area feels embedded in a clinical scan rather than pinned to a flat outline; keep the surrounding explanation and controls readable.
