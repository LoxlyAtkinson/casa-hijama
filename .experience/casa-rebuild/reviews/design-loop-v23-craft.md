## Single biggest fixable visual gap

Give the *actual rotating figure* a readable inner anatomical layer and stronger cyan rim/depth lighting, especially around the selected shoulder and back regions. Keep the warm focus localized. The present zooms enlarge a smooth shell and sparse guide lines; they should reveal materially richer interior structure while remaining tied to the same model. This is the highest-impact gap because it is visible in every front, back, mobile, and zoom state, and directly separates the current pixels from the supplied clinical reference.

Overall CRAFT verdict: **FAIL** until the body visual is repaired and freshly rendered for another independent review. This is a visual craft verdict, not a behaviour or source audit.
