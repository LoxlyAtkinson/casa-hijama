# Design Loop v30 — independent craft judgment

**Review scope:** final v30 rendered home/body evidence at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, `bar-v3.md`, the user-supplied `3d human body.jpg`, and the referenced Apple Watch Series 12 page. I did not read earlier reviews, implementation source, or builder rationale. The reviewer and builder share a filesystem; the supplied captures and my browser access cannot establish a fully separate rendering environment.

| Surface | Verdict | Visual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | **PASS** | `desktop-hero-start.png` gives one cup a dominant near-black macro frame, an oversized short headline, and an obvious booking action. `desktop-hero-room.png` changes both subject and spatial scale, while mobile `mobile-hero-start.png` and `mobile-hero-room.png` retain a readable editorial hierarchy. This follows the reference's major compositional and scene-change mechanisms without imitating its product imagery. |
| Body | **FAIL** | `desktop-body-front.png` and `mobile-body-front.png` now read as a continuous cyan clinical scan with a localized pink shoulder focus. The v30 connected lenses in `desktop-body-v30-neck-zoom.png`, `desktop-body-v30-lower-zoom.png`, `mobile-body-v30-neck-zoom.png`, and `mobile-body-v30-lower-zoom.png` make a visible change on selection, but the shoulder close view reads as bright cyan/purple fragments instead of a legible shoulder structure. The lower-back view shows a mini spine/pelvis, but on mobile the rectangular canvas tile is visible inside/behind the intended circular lens. These break the clean, anatomy-led close-detail treatment in the supplied clinical reference. |

**Largest actionable pixel gap:** Make each lens a clean circular window with no rectangular render edge, then frame the selected bone/joint at enough scale and contrast to read immediately as shoulder or lumbar anatomy. The neck/shoulder lens needs a recognizable clavicle/scapula/humeral-head relationship rather than thin neon fragments. Keep the single warm focus and leader line, which now make the lens feel connected to the live body. This is a visual craft finding; it does not assert whether WebGL rotation, scroll coupling, or questionnaire behavior works.
