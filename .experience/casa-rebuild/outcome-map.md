# Outcome map — 2026-10-17

Working rule from Eden FM process improvements 27, 37 and 62: plan and verify the visitor's verb, object and visible result across the connected journey. An attractive component or passing build is only supporting evidence.

| Visitor can… | Visible result | End-to-end proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scroll through the home story | Hero depth, section reveals and room film visibly respond, reverse on upward scroll, and yield static access under reduced motion | Browser scroll down/up on desktop and 390px mobile; capture all steps and reduced-motion state |
| Select a pain area | A genuinely rendered continuous 3D clinical body turns/zooms to the area, highlights it and shows restrained guidance | Click figure point and named control for neck, upper back, lower back; keyboard access; observe resulting orientation and content |
| Describe their concern | Three to five relevant intake answers are collected locally and lead to a clear discussion or booking action without diagnosis or treatment promise | Open, answer, complete, revisit and recover; inspect destination and data handling |
| Explore a product | The product is legible on a neutral visual stage, its factual details open, and the visitor can add it to the bag | Start at shop entry, filter/search, inspect, add, change quantity, remove, empty and reopen on desktop/mobile |
| Enquire about products | The bag produces an accurate WhatsApp enquiry with explicit external handoff and recovery | Follow populated bag through generated message and blocked-navigation fallback |
| Request treatment | The booking form validates and creates an accurate WhatsApp request, with a recovery link | Test blank and completed forms, keyboard, pending-confirmation copy, destination and fallback |
| Preview the site publicly | All routes open directly and survive reload, with current assets and motion | Verify GitHub Pages deployment, routes and rendered build after final push |

Source references are visual and interaction inspiration only: Apple Watch Series 12, the user's VITA body image, their product-showcase image and their cart/product screenshot. The website content and medical claims remain governed by the Casa Hijama source and conservative health guidance.

Five states to consider for each journey: entry/default, in-progress action, resulting success, empty/invalid or failed external/media state, and return/reversal or recovery. Do not infer success from DOM presence alone.
